# CEMENT PLANT DATA REQUEST

Dear plant, plant R&D group, or university collaborator,

We are preparing a scientific clinker-process research program focused on understanding the relationship between raw-material chemistry, raw-mix composition, kiln operation, and measured clinker quality. The goal is to build a valid and traceable dataset for scientific analysis and method validation.

We are requesting anonymized data only. We do not need company-identifying information beyond what is required to maintain the scientific relationship between upstream process records and clinker measurements.

## Preferred minimum dataset

We request the following data fields, if available:

1. Plant_ID
2. Kiln_ID
3. Raw material chemistry
4. Raw mix proportions
5. Raw_Mix_ID
6. Kiln_Run_ID
7. timestamped kiln/process variables
8. Clinker Sample_ID
9. Production_DateTime
10. Sampling_DateTime
11. clinker XRF chemistry
12. clinker Free CaO
13. clinker XRD/Rietveld phase composition
14. measurement methods
15. laboratory metadata
16. residence time or sampling delay information
17. data provenance
18. permission for academic research use

## Data requested

- Raw material chemistry: CaO, SiO2, Al2O3, Fe2O3, MgO, SO3, Na2O, K2O, LOI, moisture, and any additional relevant chemistry
- Raw mix proportions: limestone, clay, iron corrective, silica corrective, and other relevant materials
- Kiln process variables: feed rate, kiln speed, fuel rate, temperature, oxygen, CO, CO2, cooler data, and other available operating metrics
- Clinker measurements: oxide chemistry, phase composition, free CaO, sampling date, laboratory method, instrument ID, replicate ID, units, and basis
- Traceability metadata: plant ID, kiln ID, raw mix ID, kiln run ID, sample ID, production window, and sampling delay

## Research use and confidentiality

We will use the data only for research and method validation. We are not seeking unnecessary confidential information. We are especially interested in the following:

- anonymized plant IDs
- coded kiln IDs
- anonymized sample IDs
- process and chemistry tables linked by recorded IDs
- measurement methods and provenance
- research-use permission and data-use conditions

We are not requesting proprietary recipe names, exact location details, or confidential commercial operating limits unless they are scientifically necessary and can be safely anonymized.

## Preferred anonymization format

Plant and kiln names may be represented as:

- Plant_A
- Plant_B
- Kiln_1
- Kiln_2

Sample IDs may be represented as:

- Sample_0001
- Sample_0002

This preserves relationships between records while protecting confidential commercial identity.

## Sharing conditions

If available, please include:

- data use permission
- research license or terms
- whether the data can be used for academic publication or internal validation
- any restrictions on anonymized sharing

Thank you for considering this request. A scientifically valid industrial clinker dataset would materially improve the quality of the project and support a defensible traceability and materials-analysis program.
